# BLAKE3 references

## IETF Internet-Draft

`draft-aumasson-blake3-00.txt` is the July 2024 Internet-Draft titled
*The BLAKE3 Hashing Framework*, downloaded verbatim from the IETF archive:

<https://www.ietf.org/archive/id/draft-aumasson-blake3-00.txt>

As of 2026-08-05, BLAKE3 has no published RFC. This document is an expired
individual Internet-Draft, not an IETF standard or an RFC. Its status and
revision history are recorded by the IETF Datatracker:

<https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-aumasson-blake3/>

The BLAKE3 team's canonical specification and design paper is maintained
separately at:

<https://github.com/BLAKE3-team/BLAKE3-specs>
